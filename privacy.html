Privacy Policy

CoreCut · last updated 18 September 2026

Who we are

CoreCut is published by Raindrop LLC (registered as Raindrop Limited Liability Company). "We" and "us" in this policy mean Raindrop LLC. You can reach us at help@raindrop.games.

What we collect

CoreCut does not send personal information to Raindrop. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your unlocked sites, tutorial preference, saved cuts, and purchase-delivery records are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this information. Deleting the app removes this local data; previously delivered consumable cuts cannot be restored.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to verify purchases and restore permanent purchases. It does not send purchase records to Raindrop servers. Apple processes purchases under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

CoreCut does not collect personal information from children.

Support correspondence and your rights

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you provide to respond to your request. Contact help@raindrop.games to request access to, correction of, or deletion of that correspondence.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
