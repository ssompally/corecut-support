How to play

The site starts full. Tap a tile, then a cell.

  • Place a layered tile onto a core. Touching layers of the same colour combine, and surface layers can flow to matching neighbours.
  • Reach ten of one colour and that layer clears — the cell gets one layer deeper.
  • A new colour covers the old surface. Read all the layers in your tray; only clearing at ten increases recorded depth.
  • Each site gives you a limited number of cuts. Spend them well.

Common questions

I bought cuts and did not receive them.
Email help@raindrop.games with the date of purchase. Cut packs are consumables; Restore Purchases does not replenish previously delivered cuts.
Will I lose progress if I reinstall?
Deleting the app removes its local data. Restore Purchases applies to permanent purchases, not previously delivered consumable cut packs. Contact us before deleting the app if you need help with a purchase.
A site seems impossible.
Every site is verified solvable before release. If one has you stuck, tell us the site number — that feedback genuinely shapes the next update.
